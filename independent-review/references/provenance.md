# Provenance for independent-review

Adapted sources (ideas re-expressed here in new wording):

- Anthropic, *Claude Code best practices*
  (https://code.claude.com/docs/en/best-practices): the adversarial
  review step before treating work as done, splitting author from
  critic, restricting a reviewer's brief to what changes correctness,
  requirements, security, or operability, and worktrees for parallel
  sessions.
- akonev, *A skill is more than just a prompt* (Oct 2026)
  (https://discourse.canonical.com/t/a-skill-is-more-than-just-a-prompt/8251):
  state checks and routing probes, elaborated in skill-review.md.

Original to this repository: the probe lists, the self-review report
contract ("probes fired" and the independent-reviewer line),
commissioning reviews against the goal rather than a claim checklist,
the ratchet with its two counterweights, and the five plan-defect
classes.
